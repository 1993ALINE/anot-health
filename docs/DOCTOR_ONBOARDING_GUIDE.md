# ANOT HEALTH - QUICK START GUIDE FOR DOCTORS

## Welcome! 👋

ANOT Health is your AI-powered medical scribe. Record patient visits, and AI generates notes automatically.

---

## LOGIN

1. Go to: https://app.anot.health
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"
4. You'll see the dashboard

**First time?** Contact support to get your credentials.

---

## RECORD A VISIT

### Step 1: Create a Patient
1. Click **"New Patient"** (if not exists)
2. Enter patient name, DOB, medical record #
3. Click **"Create"**

### Step 2: Start Recording
1. Click **"New Visit"** 
2. Select the patient
3. Click **"Start Recording"** (red button)
4. Speak naturally — AI listens

### Step 3: Stop & Review
1. Click **"Stop Recording"** 
2. Audio uploads automatically
3. AI generates draft note (1-2 minutes)
4. Review the note for accuracy

---

## SUBMIT NOTES

1. Review AI-generated note
2. Edit if needed (fix any errors)
3. Click **"Approve Note"**
4. Note is now in patient chart
5. QPS team may grade quality

---

## OFFLINE MODE 📱

No internet? No problem!

1. Recording works offline
2. Audio uploads when you reconnect
3. AI generates notes automatically
4. You don't need to do anything

---

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### "I can't login"
→ Check your email/password
→ Reset password: https://app.anot.health/forgot-password

### "Recording not working"
→ Check microphone permissions (allow access)
→ Refresh page and try again
→ Contact support

### "Note didn't generate"
→ Wait 2-3 minutes (AI is processing)
→ Refresh page
→ Contact support

### "Internet connection lost"
→ App works offline
→ Recording continues
→ Notes upload when reconnected
