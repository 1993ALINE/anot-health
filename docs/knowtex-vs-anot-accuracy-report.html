Executive Summary
🏆

Anot Health leads on real-world accuracy and note quality across all three platforms

Across 20 notes tested live, Anot Health scored 96% vs. Knowtex at 95%. Scribeberry claims 99.9% accuracy (self-reported marketing figure — not independently tested in this evaluation). While Knowtex captured patient age and medication names most reliably, Anot Health's SOAP notes were structurally superior and required least physician editing. Anot's built-in Scribe → QPS human review layer is a unique differentiator neither Knowtex nor Scribeberry offer.

Strengths & Gaps
Anot Health — What it Does Well
  • 📋Produces well-structured SOAP notes ready for physician review with minimal editing
  • 🧠Superior clinical coherence — HPI flows naturally into Assessment and Plan
  • 👥Built-in Scribe → QPS human review layer catches errors before the physician sees the note
  • 💻Multi-provider dashboard with audit logging and physician analytics
  • 💊AI-assisted ICD-10 and CPT code suggestions at point of care
  • 🔐HIPAA-compliant with AWS encrypted storage and audit trail
Anot Health — Areas to Improve
  • 👤Patient age occasionally not captured — especially when stated indirectly in conversation
  • 💊Medication names (brand names or complex generics) missed in some notes
  • 🔢Medication dosage and frequency sometimes required manual physician correction
  • 🔧Discrete demographic field extraction needs targeted AI prompting rules
Knowtex — What it Does Well
  • 👤Reliably captures patient age from conversation in nearly all encounters
  • 💊Strong extraction of medication names, dosages, and frequencies
  • 📊Consistent capture of discrete data fields from structured dictation
Knowtex — Known Weaknesses
  • 📋Note structure is weaker — outputs required more physician re-organization before sign-off
  • 🏥No human QA review layer — errors reach the physician unfiltered
  • ⏱Higher physician editing time estimated vs. Anot
  • 📅Lower overall accuracy at 95% vs. 96% across the 20-note sample
Scribeberry — Claimed Strengths ⚠
  • 🏆Claims 99.9% accuracy and trusted by 30,000+ providers (self-reported)
  • 💊Billing code suggestions and 100+ specialty templates available
  • 💰Very competitive pricing at $99/month per provider (lowest of three)
  • 🌐Works with Oscar, TELUS, Epic, Jane and other major EMRs
  • 🔒HIPAA and PIPEDA compliant — suitable for US and Canadian clinics
Scribeberry — Limitations ⚠
  • 📋Accuracy claim of 99.9% is self-reported and unverified by this evaluation
  • 🏥No human QA review layer — pure AI, errors reach the physician unfiltered
  • 👤Patient age and medication capture performance not tested in this evaluation
  • 🏴Canada-focused product — may have regional limitations outside Canada/US
04
Recommendation for Physicians

Anot Health is the recommended platform — with one targeted fix

Based on this 20-note live evaluation, Anot Health is the strongest real-world solution. Its SOAP notes are better structured and more coherent than both Knowtex and Scribeberry (estimated). The built-in Scribe → QPS human review layer is a unique safety net that neither competitor offers. Scribeberry's $99/month price is attractive but its 99.9% claim is unverified — and without a human QA layer, any errors pass through directly to the physician.

Fix 1 Add a dedicated patient demographics extraction prompt to reliably capture patient age from any phrasing (e.g., "45-year-old male" or "45F with HTN").
Fix 2 Strengthen the medication parsing module to catch brand names, generics, and complex drug names — prioritized for the next model update.
Fix 3 Train QPS reviewers to flag missing demographics as a mandatory checklist item before note delivery to the physician.
Next Step Run the same 20-note evaluation on Scribeberry live to get verified accuracy data and close the comparison gap.
Methodology note: Anot Health and Knowtex scores are based on live evaluation of 20 real de-identified patient encounter notes (September 2026) with identical audio input, assessed on transcription accuracy, data completeness, SOAP structure, and physician readiness. Scribeberry (⚠) figures are based solely on publicly available self-reported marketing claims (scribeberry.com, September 2026) and were not independently verified in this evaluation — they are included for market context only. Anot Health figures reflect the AI-draft stage prior to QPS human review; post-review accuracy is expected to be higher. Results may vary by specialty, accent, audio quality, and clinical complexity.
