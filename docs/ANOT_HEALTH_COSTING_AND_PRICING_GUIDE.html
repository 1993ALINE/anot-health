3. Unit Economics & Monthly Cost Reduction

Cost per Patient Visit

With silence trimming and prompt caching, complete ambient transcription and clinical SOAP note generation costs only $0.09 CAD (under 9 cents CAD per visit), down from $0.21 CAD.

Cost per Doctor per Day

Supporting a physician seeing 30 patients across a full clinic day costs just $2.69 CAD / day in total AI and cloud infrastructure, down from $6.19 CAD/day.

Net Monthly Savings

Cost optimization saves $2,728.05 CAD every month ($32,736 CAD annually), while preserving identical clinical note quality and turnaround speed.

Key Cost-Reduction Finding: Deepgram audio transcription represents 86.9% of all operational spend. In clinical appointments, actual doctor-patient dialogue is only 12–15 minutes (the remainder is silent physical exams and chart review). By applying adaptive silence trimming (cutting dead air) and switching to Deepgram's Growth tier ($0.0036/min), Deepgram spend drops from $4,195.85 CAD down to $1,638.00 CAD, delivering the vast majority of savings automatically.
