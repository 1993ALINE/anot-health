The Time Burn Problem

At 30 patients a day with 30-minute consultations, doctors are working 15 hours of patient care daily. Charting notes manually would require an extra 2.5 to 3 hours every night per doctor. Across 30 doctors, that is 75 to 90 hours of overtime charting every day.

The Clinic ROI

Anot Health generates the full SOAP note in real-time as the visit concludes. By saving 2.5 hours per doctor daily across 24 days, you reclaim 1,800 hours of clinical time per month. Valued at $150/hr, that represents $270,000 in monthly clinical value for an investment of only $11,970.
